Hermes Agent – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Hermes Agent is a personal application used only by its owner (GitHub user bmat76) on their own computer. It is not offered to anyone else.

Data accessed

With the owner's consent, the app requests read-only access to the owner's Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Contacts and Google Drive (scopes: gmail.readonly, calendar.readonly, contacts.readonly, drive.readonly). It cannot send email or modify events, contacts or files.

Use and storage

Data is read only on the owner's request, to answer the owner's questions (for example, summarizing the owner's schedule or email). The OAuth token is stored only on the owner's computer. Google user data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Parts of the data may be sent to the AI model provider the owner has configured, only to process the owner's own requests.

The use of information received from Google APIs complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account → Third-party apps.
